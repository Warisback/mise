The rules

You write the rules once. The code enforces them.

The model can't talk its way past a rule. Anything outside them waits for you.

SP-1Approved suppliers onlyYour fishmonger, greengrocer and dairy. Nobody else gets paid.
SP-2 · SP-3£150 and under goes throughAnything bigger waits for one tap from you.
SP-4 · SP-5Hard ceilingsNever more than £600 an order or £800 a day.
SP-7Never pay more than last timeIt haggles down, never up.
SP-8Buy for tomorrow, not the binForecast need plus 5%, never more than 2 days' cover.
SP-9The card is the purchase orderCapped at the agreed total, so overcharges get declined.
Your stock

Old school or new school. Both work.

Pen and paper

Snap your stock count.

Write what's left in the walk-in on any scrap of paper and take a photo. Mise reads it, updates what's on hand and redoes tonight's order.

Inventory system

Or connect your inventory.

If you already track stock in an inventory or till system, Mise reads your menu and stock levels from it, sees what's missing and orders the difference.
